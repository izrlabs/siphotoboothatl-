We Create Unforgettable Moments

SI Photo Booth was born from a simple belief — every celebration deserves a moment worth keeping forever. We're Atlanta's premier premier photo booth rental company, serving weddings, corporate events, proms, birthdays, quinceañeras, bar and bat mitzvahs, baby showers, sweet 16s, ribbon cutting ceremonies, graduations and more.

From Buckhead to Marietta, Alpharetta to Sandy Springs and across all of Georgia — we bring studio-quality photography, glamorous backdrops and unforgettable fun to every event we touch.
