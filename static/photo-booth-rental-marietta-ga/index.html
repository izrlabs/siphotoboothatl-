Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel to Marietta for free?

Yes! Travel to Marietta, Cobb County, and surrounding areas is included at no extra charge. Contact us for events further out for a custom quote.

How many prints do we get?

Unlimited! Every guest can print as many times as they want at no extra charge. You also receive a complete digital gallery of every photo taken.

How long does setup take?

We arrive 1 hour before your event start time for setup. A 3-hour package means 3 full hours of booth run time, not including setup.

Will someone run the booth for us?

Absolutely. A professional SI Photo Booth attendant is on-site for the entire duration of your event. They handle everything — you just enjoy your party.

Can guests share photos to their phone?

Yes! Guests can text or email photos directly from the booth to their phones instantly and post to social media right away.

Do you do events other than weddings?

We love all events! Weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, galas, quinceañeras, school events and more. Tell us about your event and we'll make it work.

How much space does the booth need?

We need at least 10x10 feet, access to one power outlet, and level ground. Backdrops require a ceiling height of at least 8 feet.
