## Tips for planning your Lawrenceville photo booth

- **Pick the spot early.** Choose a high-traffic corner away from loud speakers, with room for a short line.
- **Plan for light.** Avoid direct sun behind the booth. Shade and indoor spaces give the best photos.
- **Check power.** A standard outlet within 25 feet of the booth is ideal.
- **Match your hours to your event.** Most guests use the booth during cocktail hour, dinner and the first part of the dance floor.
- **Share the booth.** Mention it in your program or on a sign so guests know it is there.
