## Why a photo booth works for Alpharetta events

Alpharetta is one of metro Atlanta's busiest event cities. Downtown Alpharetta and the Avalon area host constant weddings, birthday dinners and company celebrations, and the tech and finance offices along the GA-400 corridor throw holiday parties and client events all year. Guests here arrive dressed up and ready for photos, which is exactly when a booth shines.

A photo booth gives every guest a reason to step away from the table, and it produces photos that look better than most phone snapshots because of the studio lighting and dedicated camera. For weddings it becomes the guest book that actually gets used. For corporate events it creates branded content that guests and clients share without being asked.

## Booth options for your Alpharetta event

### Open-air photo booth

Our most flexible option. There is no enclosure, so groups of two or ten can squeeze into the frame, and it sets up neatly in ballrooms, barns, backyards and patios. ## Tips for planning your Alpharetta photo booth

- **Pick the spot early.** Choose a high-traffic corner away from loud speakers, with room for a short line.
- **Plan for light.** Avoid direct sun behind the booth. Shade and indoor spaces give the best photos.
- **Check power.** A standard outlet within 25 feet of the booth is ideal.
- **Match your hours to your event.** Most guests use the booth during cocktail hour, dinner and the first part of the dance floor.
- **Share the booth.** Mention it in your program or on a sign so guests know it is there.
