## Booth options for your Roswell event

We bring four booth styles, and each one suits a different kind of Roswell event.

### Open-air photo booth

The open-air booth is our most flexible option. There is no enclosure, so groups of two or ten can squeeze into the frame together, and it sets up neatly in ballrooms, barns, backyards and patios. Guests get prints or digital copies, and the template at the bottom of each strip can be customized with your names, date or company logo. Open-air booths start at **$175**.

### Glam booth

The glam booth is built for portraits. Soft studio lighting and flattering framing give guests the look of a mini photo session, and many couples choose black and white for a timeless result. It works especially well at weddings, galas, bridal showers and milestone birthdays. Glam booths start at **$200**.

### Enclosed photo booth

If you want the classic feel, with a curtain, privacy and a little bit of mystery, the enclosed booth delivers it. Guests love ducking in with a friend and coming out with a strip they actually want to keep. Enclosed booths start at **$225**.

### 360 video booth

The 360 video booth is the crowd magnet. Guests stand on a platform while a camera rotates around them, creating slow motion videos that are ready to post in seconds. It is a favorite at quinceañeras, sweet 16 parties, graduations, birthdays and corporate parties. 360 booths start at **$175**.

## Tips for planning your Roswell photo booth

- **Pick the spot early.** Choose a high-traffic corner away from loud speakers, with room for a short line.
- **Plan for light.** Avoid direct sun behind the booth. Shade and indoor spaces give the best photos.
- **Check power.** A standard outlet within 25 feet of the booth is ideal.
- **Match your hours to your event.** Most guests use the booth during cocktail hour, dinner and the first part of the dance floor.
- **Share the booth.** Mention it in your program or on a sign so guests know it is there.
