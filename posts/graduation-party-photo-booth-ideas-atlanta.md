Graduation parties are a chance to celebrate the grad with family and friends, and a photo booth gives everyone a keepsake. Here are ideas for your Atlanta party.

## Make the grad the star

- A custom print template with the grad's name, school and class year.
- A backdrop in the school colors, or a simple sparkle backdrop that lets the grad stand out.
- Props like caps, signs and glasses with the class year.

## Choose a booth that fits the party

Backyard parties and halls both work. An open-air booth fits easily and keeps lines moving, while a [360 video booth](/what-is-a-360-video-booth-atlanta/) adds a fun moment that guests will share. Enclosed booths suit guests who like a more traditional feel.

## Think about the setting

If the party is outdoors, plan for shade, a level area and a power outlet nearby. If it rains, a covered area or a garage keeps the booth running. Tell us about the venue when you request a quote and we will help with the setup.

## Plan the timing

- Open the booth once most guests have arrived.
- Keep it near the food and music so guests pass by.
- Add a group photo with the grad and family.

## Book early for May and June

Graduation season is busy, and Saturdays fill early. Ask for a quote a month or two ahead so you have your pick of dates.
