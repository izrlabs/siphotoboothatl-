Not every photo booth company offers the same thing. A few good questions before you book can save you stress on the day. Use this checklist for weddings, corporate events and parties anywhere in Atlanta.

## 1. What exactly is included?

Ask whether the quote covers prints, props, the backdrop, the print design, the attendant and the digital gallery. Surprises tend to show up in the add-ons.

## 2. Are prints unlimited?

Some companies cap the number of prints. Ask whether every guest can print as many times as they like during your event.

## 3. Is there an attendant, and are they there the whole time?

A professional attendant keeps the line moving, handles props and fixes small problems fast. You should not have to run the booth yourself.

## 4. Can the print template be customized?

A custom template with your names, date or logo turns a strip of photos into a keepsake. Ask who designs it and how early they need your details.

## 5. How do guests get their digital photos?

Look for instant sharing by text, email or QR code, plus a full gallery for you after the event.

## 6. How much space and power does the booth need?

Open-air and enclosed booths usually need about a 10 by 10 foot area, and 360 platforms need more. Confirm ceiling height, floor level and access to an outlet with your venue.

## 7. When do they arrive and how long is setup?

A good company arrives early, sets up without disrupting your event and breaks down quietly afterward.

## 8. Are travel fees included for your venue?

Ask about your city and ZIP code up front. We serve Atlanta, Marietta, Alpharetta, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Lawrenceville, Kennesaw and Midtown.

## 9. What is the booking and cancellation policy?

Find out how much a deposit is, when the balance is due, and what happens if your date changes.

## 10. Can you see real examples?

Ask for photos from events like yours. Check our [gallery](/gallery/) to see our booths in action and compare styles.

## Quick checklist

- Event date, venue, city and ZIP code
- Booth type: open-air, enclosed, 360 or glam
- Print template details and branding
- Hours of service you need
- Space, power and access at your venue
