The backdrop is the first thing guests see, and it sets the look of every photo. Here is how to choose one for your Atlanta wedding or party.

## Sequin backdrops

Sequin walls are a favorite because they catch the light and make photos look glamorous. Popular colors include rose gold, black and iridescent. They suit weddings, birthdays, graduations, New Year's parties and anything with a bit of sparkle.

## Flower walls

A white flower wall gives a soft, romantic look that works especially well for weddings, bridal showers and baby showers. It photographs beautifully in daylight and in warm indoor light. The flower wall is an upgrade, so mention it when you request a quote.

## How to match the backdrop to your event

- **Wedding:** match your color palette. Rose gold and white flowers are popular, and black sequin suits a formal look.
- **Birthday:** go bold with a color that matches the guest of honor.
- **Corporate:** keep it clean, and consider a custom backdrop with your logo.
- **Graduation:** school colors or a simple sparkle backdrop that lets the grad stand out.

## Tips for better photos

1. Keep the backdrop away from windows with bright light behind it.
2. Choose a color that contrasts with the clothing guests will wear.
3. Pair it with a custom print template in a matching color.
