A quinceañera or Sweet 16 is a milestone, and the photos are part of the memory. A photo booth gives guests something to do and gives the birthday girl a keepsake. Here are ideas to make it great.

## Choose a booth for a big, active crowd

These parties usually have lots of guests and lots of energy. An open-air booth keeps lines moving and fits whole groups, from the court of honor to family. A [360 video booth](/what-is-a-360-video-booth-atlanta/) is a big hit with teens and young guests who love a slow-motion video to post.

## Match the backdrop to the theme

Pick a backdrop in the party colors. Sequin backdrops in rose gold or iridescent look especially pretty, and a flower wall gives a soft, elegant look. See the options in our [gallery](/gallery/).

## Make the print a keepsake

Ask for a custom print template with the guest of honor's name, the date and a design that matches the invitations. Guests take the strips home, and many keep them for years.

## Plan the timing

- Open the booth after the formal parts, such as the entrance, toasts and the first dance.
- Let the court take group photos first, then open it to everyone.
- Keep it near the dance floor so guests drift in and out.

## Make guests feel included

- Props that fit the theme, from crowns to signs.
- A group shot for the whole family.
- Digital sharing so guests can send photos to their phones.
