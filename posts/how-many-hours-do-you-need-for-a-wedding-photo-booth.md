One of the most common questions couples ask is how long to book the photo booth. The honest answer depends on your guest count and your timeline. Here is a simple way to decide.

## Why more hours is not always better

Guests use the photo booth most right after cocktail hour and once dinner is done. Booking it for the whole evening can mean long quiet stretches, while booking it too briefly can leave late arrivals without a turn.

## A simple guide

- **Two hours:** works for smaller weddings, around 50 to 75 guests, or if you only want the booth for part of the reception.
- **Three hours:** a good fit for most weddings. It covers the stretch after dinner when the dance floor is busy.
- **Four hours:** better for larger weddings, around 150 guests or more, or when the booth is open from cocktail hour into the dance party.

These are guidelines, not rules. A big crowd that loves photos may use the booth nonstop, and a quieter group may not.

## Think about your schedule

Look at your timeline and mark when guests are free to wander:

1. Cocktail hour, when guests are mingling.
2. After dinner, before the main dancing.
3. During the dance party, when the energy is high.

Place the booth for the time that fits best, and avoid running it during toasts, the first dance or the cake cutting.

## Tips to get more out of it

- Ask your DJ or emcee to announce the booth.
- Place it near the dance floor and the bar, but away from speakers.
- Choose a [backdrop](/gallery/) that matches your colors.
- Add a custom print template with your names and date.
