December is the busiest month for parties in Atlanta, and a photo booth is one of the easiest ways to keep a holiday crowd happy. Whether it is an office party, a family gathering or a neighborhood celebration, here is how to plan one that guests remember.

## Book early, because December dates go fast

Weekends in December are the first to fill, especially the Fridays and Saturdays before the holidays. If you have a date in mind, ask for a quote now. Weekday and early-December parties are often easier to fit, so be flexible if you can.

## Pick a backdrop that photographs well

A sparkly backdrop turns a plain room into a party. Sequin backdrops catch the light and look great in photos, and a flower wall gives a softer, elegant look. Tell us your colors and we will recommend a backdrop that fits. See our [gallery](/gallery/) for the backdrops we use.

## Choose the right booth for the room

- **Open-air booth:** takes little space, keeps lines moving and fits groups of friends or coworkers.
- **Enclosed booth:** feels like a classic photo booth and suits guests who like a little privacy.
- **360 video booth:** the showstopper for holiday parties. Guests step onto the platform and get a slow-motion video they will post right away.

Not sure which one? Read our guide to [open-air, enclosed and 360 booths](/open-air-vs-enclosed-vs-360-booth-atlanta-wedding/).

## Add a holiday touch

- A custom print template with your party name, date or company logo.
- Seasonal props such as hats, glasses and signs.
- A shared hashtag so guests can post and find each other's photos.

## Time it for when guests are most social

Photo booths are busiest right after people arrive and again once dinner is over. If your party runs four hours, plan the booth for the stretch when most guests are there, not the first thirty minutes.
