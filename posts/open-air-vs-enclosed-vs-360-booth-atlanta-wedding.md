Booking a photo booth is one of those wedding decisions that looks simple until you see the options. Open-air, enclosed, 360, glam: they all deliver fun, but they create very different moments. Here is how to pick the one that fits your Atlanta wedding.

## Open-air photo booth: best for groups and elegant venues

An open-air booth has no walls, just a professional camera, lighting and a backdrop of your choice. That means a whole bridal party, or a family of ten, can squeeze into one frame.

**Choose it if:**

- You want big group photos, not just couples and pairs
- Your venue is formal and you want a clean, elegant look
- You want guests to watch each other's poses and cheer them on

Our [open-air booth](/services/) includes unlimited instant prints, a custom print template, a premium backdrop you pick and a professional attendant who runs everything.

## Enclosed photo booth: best for a classic, private feel

An enclosed booth is our inflatable enclosure with an LED look and a red carpet entrance. Guests step inside, close the curtain and get a little privacy, which tends to bring out the funny, spontaneous shots.

**Choose it if:**

- You love the traditional photo-strip booth experience
- Your venue has a lot of ambient light and you want a controlled setup
- You expect guests who prefer a quieter, more private moment

## 360 video booth: best for energy and social media

A 360 booth is a platform where guests stand while a camera arm circles them, capturing slow-motion video. Guests leave with a short clip they can share right away.

**Choose it if:**

- You want a high-energy moment on the dance floor
- Your guests love posting to Instagram and TikTok
- You have enough room for the platform (we confirm space with you before the day)

## Glam booth: best for a magazine-cover moment

Our glam booth adds beauty-dish lighting, a wind machine effect and a black-and-white option. It is a favorite for couples who want striking, editorial-style portraits.

## How to decide

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **What does my venue allow?** Ceiling height, floor space and power access matter. Tell us your venue and we will confirm what fits.
2. **How many guests will use it?** Larger groups suit open-air; couples and small friend groups love enclosed and glam.
3. **What do I want my guests to remember?** Printed keepsakes, shareable videos or both?

Many couples pair a booth with a keepsake plan, such as a guest-book page of printed strips, so the photos live on after the night.
