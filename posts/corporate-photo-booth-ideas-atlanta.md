A photo booth at a corporate event does two jobs at once: it gives your guests something fun to do, and it puts your brand in every photo they share. Here are seven ideas that work for Atlanta company parties, galas, conferences and product launches.

## 1. Custom logo on every print

Every printed strip and digital photo can carry your logo, event name and date. Guests take the prints home, and the digital copies carry your brand on social media. Design is included, so send us your logo and colors ahead of time.

## 2. A branded backdrop

A step-and-repeat or custom backdrop makes the photos look like part of your event, not a random corner of the room. It also gives your photos a consistent look across hundreds of guests.

## 3. A 360 video booth for the "wow" moment

A [360 video booth](/services/) is a showstopper at launches and holiday parties. Executives and new hires alike end up in the same slow-motion clip, which breaks the ice faster than a networking icebreaker. Add your logo overlay and a custom song to match the event.

## 4. A branded hashtag and instant sharing

Guests can send photos by email, text or QR code right from the booth. Add your event hashtag to the print template and you have an easy way to collect photos on social media.

## 5. Props that fit the theme

Generic props are fine, but themed props are better: company slogans, industry jokes, or holiday items for a December party. Small details make people laugh and pose.

## 6. A team-building twist

Run a friendly challenge: each department gets a photo-strip template and a theme, or guests collect strips from different booths around the event. It keeps people moving and mingling.

## 7. Keep the photo gallery afterward

After the event, you get the full gallery of digital photos. Teams can use them in the company newsletter, on your website and on social channels, long after the party ends.
