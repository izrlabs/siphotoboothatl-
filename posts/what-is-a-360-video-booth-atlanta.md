If you have seen a dramatic slow-motion video of friends spinning at a party, you have seen a 360 video booth in action. Here is what it is, how it works and what to know before you book one for your Atlanta event.

## What is a 360 video booth?

A 360 video booth is a platform where one or more guests stand while a camera arm rotates around them. The result is a smooth, cinematic video that circles the subject, often played in slow motion with a speed-up for effect, set to music.

## How it works at your event

1. Guests step onto the platform, and a few can fit at once.
2. An attendant starts the recording, and the arm circles the group.
3. The video is edited on the spot with your chosen overlay and music.
4. Guests can share it instantly by text, email or QR code.

## How much space does it need?

Plan for about a 12 by 12 foot area, with level ground and access to a power outlet. We confirm the requirements for your venue ahead of time, so there are no surprises on the day.

## 360 booth or traditional photo booth?

They are different experiences. A traditional [open-air or enclosed booth](/services/) gives guests prints to take home. A 360 booth gives them a video to post. If your budget and space allow, many hosts book both.

## Tips for the best videos

- Put the booth where there is room for onlookers, since the crowd adds to the energy
- Choose a song that matches your crowd
- Add your names or logo overlay so the clips carry your event with them
- Let guests use props, because it makes the videos more fun
