Photo booth prices vary a lot, and two quotes that look different may not include the same things. Here is what to expect in Atlanta and how to compare quotes fairly.

## What people typically pay

Published guides put the national average for a photo booth rental at roughly $400 to $500. Wedding site WeddingWire lists an average of $650, with a typical range of $425 to $1,000, and The Bash shows an average of about $499 for Atlanta. Prices depend on the booth, the length of the rental and the date.

At SI Photo Booth, packages start at $175 for an open-air booth, with enclosed, 360 and glam booths priced separately. [See current prices](/services/).

## What changes the price

- **Type of booth:** enclosed and 360 booths cost more because they are larger and need more equipment.
- **Length of the rental:** longer rentals cost more overall, but the cost per hour usually goes down.
- **Date and season:** Saturdays, holidays and wedding season are the busiest and the most in demand.
- **Distance:** venues farther from our base may include a travel fee. Ask about it before you book.
- **Extras:** a custom backdrop, a guest book, prints sized differently from the standard strip, or a branded template can add to the total.

## What to check before you compare quotes

1. Is an attendant included for the whole rental?
2. Are prints and digital sharing included?
3. Is the print design customized for you?
4. Is travel included for your venue?
5. Is sales tax included in the price?

A lower quote that leaves out the attendant or prints can cost more by the end. Our checklist of [questions to ask before booking](/questions-to-ask-before-booking-a-photo-booth/) covers the rest.

## How to save without cutting the fun

- Book a weekday or an off-peak date.
- Pick the hours that match when guests are there, not the whole day.
- Book early so you have your pick of dates.
