If you have seen a photo booth that makes everyone look polished, with soft lighting and a magazine feel, you were probably looking at a glam booth. Here is how it works and when to choose one.

## What a glam booth is

A glam booth is a photo booth built for flattering portraits. It uses soft, even lighting and a clean look so guests look their best. Think studio-style photos at an event, rather than a quick snapshot.

## Who it suits

- Weddings and bridal showers, where guests dress up.
- Birthdays, galas and award nights.
- Corporate events that want a polished look for headshots or group photos.
- Any event where guests like a more elegant style.

## How it differs from an open-air booth

An open-air booth is a flexible setup with a backdrop and a camera, and it is great for big groups and fast lines. A glam booth focuses on portrait quality and a more refined look. If your guests love dressing up, a glam booth is a good choice.

## Tips to get the best results

1. Choose a backdrop that matches your colors.
2. Keep the booth away from bright windows so the lighting stays even.
3. Add a custom print template for a finished look.
